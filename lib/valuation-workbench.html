MD Fit valuation workbench

Follow an appraiser's process step by step. Each step shows its result, and the chart updates as you go. The starting figures are a hypothetical scenario, so replace them with numbers from your books.

The process

What a credentialed appraiser does, in order.
  1. 1Define the assignmentValuation date, standard of value (usually fair market value: a willing buyer and seller, neither forced), premise, level of value and purpose.
  2. 2Gather the records2–3 years of tax returns, profit and loss, balance sheet, Stripe history, debts, lease and equipment. Lenders reconcile revenue to deposits.
  3. 3Normalize earningsRestate the books to true earnings: add back owner pay, depreciation, interest, taxes and one-time costs to reach SDE; subtract a market salary to reach EBITDA.
  4. 4Account for obligationsPayments a buyer inherits, like the developer's revenue share, reduce the earnings being valued.
  5. 5Income approachBuild a discount rate from five risks, subtract growth, and capitalize next year's earnings.
  6. 6Market approachPrice the earnings with multiples from real sales of similar businesses.
  7. 7Asset approachWhat it would cost to replace the equipment and the software, less wear and age. Usually a floor.
  8. 8ReconcileWeight the three results by how reliable each is for this business, and say why.
  9. 9Partial-stake discountsA small stake is worth less than its share: discounts for lack of control and lack of marketability.
  10. 10Lender testSBA SOP 50 10 8.1: historical earnings only, 1.25x debt service coverage for owner buyouts, and the valuation caps the loan.
  11. 11Report and sign-offA written conclusion of value signed by an ASA, ABV or CVA, stating every assumption.
Standards: AICPA VS Section 100, NACVA and ASA business valuation standards, USPAP Standards 9–10, IRS Revenue Ruling 59-60, and SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 for lending.

Formulas used on this page

SDE = net revenue − operating and platform costs − revenue share + owner pay + one-time, personal and non-cash add-backs. EBITDA = SDE − market salary for an operator.

Income: earnings × (1 + g) ÷ (r − g), where r = risk-free + equity risk premium + size + industry + company-specific. Market: SDE × multiple. Asset: tangible net assets + software replacement cost × (1 − obsolescence).

Reconciled value: weighted average of the three. Partial stake: pro-rata value × (1 − DLOC) × (1 − DLOM). Revenue-share stream: annual share × (1 + g) ÷ (r − g). Supportable loan: (EBITDA ÷ coverage) turned into principal at the loan rate and term, capped at the reconciled value.

Not an appraisal, legal opinion or financial advice. Starting figures are hypothetical. Discount rates, multiples and discounts must come from a credentialed appraiser using current market data and filed financial statements.
